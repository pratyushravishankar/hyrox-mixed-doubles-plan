MonDone · 5K run

Completed: about 37 minutes.

TueErg + core

Row 5 min at RPE 5–6; 1 min easy transition; SkiErg 5 min at RPE 5–6; 1 min easy transition; row 5 min at RPE 5–6. Then, if feeling good, 2 rounds: 12 cable crunches, 8 dead bugs/side and a 30–45 sec plank. Erg work is 15 min, 17 min including transitions; steady rather than a test.

WedLower-body strength + finisher

Bulgarian split squat 3×8/side at a familiar load; hip thrust 3×10; Smith RDL 3×8–10; wall balls 2×15 at 6 kg; bent-knee Copenhagen hold 2×20 sec/side. Leave 2–3 good reps in reserve. Finisher: 2,000 m row at RPE 7, hard but controlled and evenly paced.

ThuPilates

Pilates class. Let this be the day’s workout; no extra leg session needed.

FriPartner HYROX · 3 rounds

Warm up together for 5–8 min. Each round: treadmill run together for 5 min at individually controlled speeds; row 300 m Pratyush / 200 m Yuthika, you-go-I-go (alternate who starts each round); sled push 12.5 m and pull 12.5 m each, alternating; wall balls 30 sec each, alternating fixed turns. Rest 2 min between rounds. Yuthika uses 6 kg; Pratyush uses a familiar repeatable load. Provisional sled loads per turn: Pratyush push 152 kg / pull 103 kg; Yuthika push 120 kg / pull 85 kg. Adjust if the sled surface or movement quality calls for it. Aim for RPE 7, not an all-out sim.
